Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Merge Champion ("the App", "we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you play our mobile game on Android or iOS. By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

The App is operated by Jakob Emilander, Uppsala, Sweden. As a Swedish/EU-based developer, this policy is written in compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the requirements of the Google Play Store and Apple App Store.

4

How We Use Data

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • ✓ Operating the public leaderboard (username + score)
  • ✓ Validating and restoring in-app purchases
  • ✓ Serving the daily puzzle seed via Cloudflare Workers
  • ✓ Saving game progress locally on your device

We do not use your data for advertising, tracking across apps, or profiling.

The legal basis under GDPR for processing your data is legitimate interest (operating the game, preventing abuse) and, for optional features like the public leaderboard, your explicit consent given at the point of score submission.

5

Data Retention

Data stored on Supabase (EU region) is retained for as long as the App is operational, or until you request deletion. Leaderboard entries with your username are publicly visible.

Local device data (your high score, daily progress, and settings) is stored on your device using iOS/Android secure storage. Uninstalling the App removes this data.

We will delete your Supabase-stored data within 30 days of a verified deletion request. See Your Rights below.

Your Rights (GDPR)

Under GDPR, you have the right to:

  • 📋

    Access

    Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.

  • ✏️

    Rectification

    Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.

  • 🗑️

    Erasure ("Right to be forgotten")

    Request deletion of your data from our systems.

  • ⏸️

    Restriction

    Request that we limit how we use your data.

  • 📦

    Portability

    Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.

  • 🚫

    Objection

    Object to our processing of your data where we rely on legitimate interest.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email in the Contact section. We will respond within 30 days.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Swedish supervisory authority: Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten (IMY).

7

Children's Privacy

Merge Champion is a general-audience puzzle game rated for players of all ages. The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU) without verifiable parental consent.

The optional leaderboard feature — which involves submitting a username — is an opt-in action. Children should only submit a score with parental permission.

If you believe a child has provided personal data without appropriate consent, please contact us immediately and we will delete it promptly.
